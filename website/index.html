Real-Time Operations

Dispatch Console

The nerve center of your operations. A daily view that splits arrivals and departures, with Excel-like inline editing and real-time conflict validation.

  • Split assignment — dispatchers handle vehicles, online operators handle reps
  • Vehicle fleet overview with automatic time-overlap conflict detection
  • 48-hour time-lock with manual unlock for authorized users
  • Permission-based access — each role sees only what they can control
Booking Management

Traffic Jobs

Manage every booking from creation to completion. Support for 12+ service types, bulk Excel import, and full audit trail.

  • B2B (agent) and online (direct) booking channels
  • Auto-generated internal references (ITT-XXXX)
  • Pax count validated against vehicle capacity
  • Multi-currency collection amounts with exchange rates
Financial Control

Finance & Odoo Exports

Complete invoicing lifecycle with Egyptian tax compliance and direct Odoo ERP compatibility — no customization required on the Odoo side.

  • Invoice lifecycle: Draft → Posted (immutable) → Paid
  • Automatic invoice generation per agent cycle (weekly, biweekly, monthly)
  • Five Odoo-ready Excel exports with external ID mapping
  • Driver fees, rep fees, and supplier costs tracked per trip
Enterprise Security

Roles & Permissions

Granular role-based access control with 13 system roles. Every page, action, and column is permission-aware.

  • 13 pre-configured system roles with customizable permissions
  • Field-level access control (e.g., assign vehicles vs. assign reps)
  • Full audit trail on every create, update, and delete action
  • JWT authentication with automatic refresh token rotation

Dedicated Portals

Drivers, reps, and suppliers get their own mobile-friendly portals with real-time GPS tracking and status updates.

Driver Portal

View daily assignments, update trip status with live GPS location, submit no-show evidence with geo-tagged photos, and track earnings.

Rep Portal

Track flight assignments, update job status with GPS verification, submit evidence for no-show cases, and view fee history.

Supplier Portal

External transport providers manage their own fleet, drivers, and pricing. Self-service resource management with full visibility.
